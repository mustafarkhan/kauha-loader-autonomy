Prototype of the remote supervisor's station: one person watches several machines, answers their requests and holds stop authority over each (Machinery Regulation, Annex III 3.2.4). The three machines play back shifts recorded in the KAUHA digital twin. Your stop, e-stop, reset and teleop commands act on the playback and are added to the log with their own hashes; they do not run the twin.
