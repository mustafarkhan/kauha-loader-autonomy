## Notes

- **V-STD-01** (pass): margin 5 %. The winter planning value (1.50 m/s2) is the available friction on a slippery surface, not the brake's capacity, so it is not compared here. The brake itself has to be measured on the machine.
- **V-STD-02** (open): The limit formula is unreadable in the free sample. A commonly quoted form (v2/150 + 0.2(v + 5), v in km/h) would give 7.6 m, which the twin's assumed braking would miss. Confirm the formula in the purchased standard, then measure the real brake.
- **V-STD-03** (pass): The fields are sized for the machine's stopping distance, as ISO 3691-4 does; a person walking into a machine that has already stopped is outside the stop function.
- **V-STD-04** (fail): Missed at 1 degree resolution: fwd_3.0_S/front at (8.82, 1.89); fwd_3.0_S/front at (8.82, -1.89). The fields are measured from the bucket's front edge, but the scanners sit about 3 m behind it: the far edge is up to 8.1 m from the nearest scanner, beyond the 4 m protective range the speed cap assumes. The twin's scanners see 15 m, so the shifts never showed it. The full machine needs a rated sensor with that range (or one mounted further forward), or a lower speed cap.
- **V-STD-05** (finding): A horizontal scan plane at 0.5 m passes over a 70 mm lying test piece, so a person lying on the ground is not detected by the safety scanners. ISO 3691-4 is written for indoor trucks with low scanners. Options: a second, low scan plane; a rated 3D sensor; or stating it as a residual risk covered by site access control. Not yet decided in the safety concept.
- **V-STD-06** (pass): From CAD mass properties; weigh the built machine to confirm.
- **V-STD-07** (open): Needs MTTFd, DC and CCF from the data sheets of the selected safety relay, relays and scanners (SISTEMA or hand calculation).
- **V-PUB-01** (pass): Simulated bucket-pile model against a field result on a larger machine (L180H); it shows the policy is in the published range, not that the model is right.
- **V-PUB-02** (pass): Covers the published sim-to-real force error as a change in digging resistance only.
- **V-PUB-03** (open): The operator study reports cycle times only in plots. Needs the figures digitised or the pilot site's own operator timing.
- **V-PUB-04** (needs data): The recording's channel list is in its documentation PDF, which could not be read from the build environment. The replay will be written against that list, not guessed.
- **V-DAT-01** (fail): The CRC and alive counter detect faults, not attackers: they use no secret, so anyone on the bus can forge frames. The stop itself survives (the PLC cuts power through relays, not over CAN), but a forged RemoteHeartbeat could hide a lost link or a pressed remote e-stop. The bus needs message authentication (for example AUTOSAR SecOC with a CMAC) or physical separation of the safety segment before it leaves a closed test area. Suspension is handled.
- **V-DAT-02** (needs data): Set KAUHA_DATA_ROAD to a folder of candump logs.
- **V-DAT-03** (needs data): Set KAUHA_DATA_SODA to a YOLO-layout copy (images/, labels/).
- **V-DAT-04** (needs data): Set KAUHA_DATA_LIDARDUSTX to the dataset folder (research-use licence).
- **V-DAT-05** (open): Needs the dataset's calibration and label format in hand before a loader is written.
- **V-DAT-06** (open): Radar replay needs the radar's role fixed first (detection only, or part of a field).
- **V-DAT-07** (open): Needs the benchmark's file format and the EKF run from logged GNSS/IMU instead of the twin.
